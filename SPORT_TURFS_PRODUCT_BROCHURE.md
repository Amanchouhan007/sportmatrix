## 🔄 How It Works (5 Simple Steps)

1. **Register Turf**: Set up location, grounds, floodlights & amenities.
2. **Configure Rates**: Define regular/peak pricing, slot duration & sports.
3. **Go Live & Lock**: Customers pick slots online with 5-minute atomic holds.
4. **Split Payments**: Teams pay via Dare Match, 50:50, or per-player UPI.
5. **Analyze & Scale**: Track POS revenue, court occupancy & payouts.
