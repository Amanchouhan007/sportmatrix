# SportMatrix — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name
SportMatrix — Premium Sports + Gaming Ecosystem SaaS

---

# Product Vision

SportMatrix is a premium Sports + Gaming ecosystem platform designed for:

- Turf Businesses
- Gaming Lounges
- VR Arenas
- Sports Complexes
- Gaming Cafes
- Tournament Organizers

The platform combines:

- Sports Turf Booking
- Gaming Zone Management
- POS Billing
- Membership Systems
- Tournament Management
- SaaS Subscription Management
- Role-Based Operations

into one unified enterprise ecosystem.

---
